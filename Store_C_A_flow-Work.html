1. The Executive Challenge

Large-scale US retail enterprises handle complex spatial and inventory allocation matrices . Previously, merchandising specialists and business analysts relied on disconnected legacy spreadsheets to manually cluster stores and push assortment updates .

This disjointed approach led to margin leakage, excessive markdowns, and a total lack of end-to-end status visibility across regional nodes .

Core Objective

Design a unified, high-performance analytics interface—The Store Clustering Analytics Suite—that allows analysts to seamlessly initialize projects, review automated matrix models, and confidently deploy optimized localized assortments to production environments .

3. User Research & Contextual Inquiry

We conducted contextual inquiry sessions with 8 internal retail analysts to evaluate real-world workflow barriers in their natural desk environments .

Observation & Data Gathering ("What")

Documented heavy reliance on fragmented tools (Excel, Word, SAP, legacy main s) . Mapped digital workflows and environmental pressures influencing fast-paced planning decisions .

Analysis & Debriefs ("Why")

Uncovered internal heuristics and tacit knowledge used for complex decisions . Validated findings directly with planners to confirm root friction factors .

4. Problem Framing & Friction Points

The "Black Box" Deployment Anxiety

Because data was manually shared via email, users lacked visibility into how cluster logic was routed into downstream execution systems, causing hesitation during final handoffs .

Status & Configuration Ambiguity

Managing live, draft, and approved configurations across scattered attachments frequently led to teams working out of outdated files and duplicating efforts .

5. Design Strategy & Intuitive User Flow

Core Ideation Principles:
1. Structured Information Architecture: Replaced scattered files with a linear 3-phase workspace (Initialization → Assortment Configuration → Forecast Review) .
2. High-Contrast Dark Theme & Macro KPI Bar: Designed specifically to reduce optical fatigue during long analytics sessions while keeping pipeline velocity prominent .
3. Progressive Disclosure Canvas: Transformed massive flat tables into interactive views using scannable status badges and structured inline actions .
